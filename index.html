Company overview  ·  September 2026

SmartHeal turns the clinical device already in the clinician's hand into a clinical grade assessment tool. The assessment, the measurement, the photos and the evidence based record are done in seconds instead of 25 minutes.

No hardware. No attachments. No calibration stickers. The clinician reviews and accepts every output.

The problem

  • Chronic wounds affect more than 450,000 Australians and cost the health system A$9.8B a year.
  • Wounds are still measured by hand with a paper ruler, so two clinicians assessing the same wound record two different sizes.
  • Without an objective baseline, nobody can tell whether a wound is healing, and 85% of assessments end up clinically inappropriate.
  • The cost of getting it wrong is stalled healing, avoidable hospital admission and preventable amputation.

The solution

STEP 1
Photograph
One photo on a standard clinical device, at the bedside or in the community.
STEP 2
Measure
AI returns length, width, area and perimeter, plus a structured TIME assessment.
STEP 3
Clinician accepts
The clinician edits or overrides anything, then accepts. Nothing is saved without them.
STEP 4
Record
Report generates for handover and audit, and the healing trajectory builds over time.
  • Hardware free. The established products need a bracket, a marker sticker or a dedicated device. We need none of them.
  • Human in the loop by design. Explainable output, full clinician override, complete audit trail.
  • Data handling. Encrypted, with tenant isolation, hosted onshore.
  • Currently selling as a clinical decision support system. Patent pending.
